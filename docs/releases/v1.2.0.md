# v1.2.0

You can now log in on machines with no browser, plus a few rotation fixes.

## What's new
- Manual login with `codex-multi-auth login --manual`. Finish the sign-in somewhere else and paste the result back. Handy when a browser can't open or the local callback listener won't work.
- Proxy-compatible upstream transport, so it works behind a corporate proxy.
- Accounts with a disabled or expired workspace now rotate away automatically instead of failing over and over.
- Onboarding can restore from your most recent saved backup.

## Fixes
- Explicit no-browser environment toggles are respected now.
- Manual mode no longer waits for a callback that will never arrive.
- Your chosen workspace now sticks through retries. Before, a retry or fallback could quietly move you to a different workspace.
